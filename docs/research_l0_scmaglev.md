# Research Dossier: JR Central L0 Series SCMaglev & Chūō Shinkansen

## 1. Candidate Selection — Why the L0 Series SCMaglev

We select the **JR Central L0 Series SCMaglev** (specifically the **Improved L0 Series / 950-series**, introduced March 2020) as the subject of *"500 km/h Through Japan"*. On 21 April 2015, a 7-car L0 Series set achieved **603 km/h** on the 42.8 km Yamanashi Maglev Line (sustaining 603 km/h for 10.8 seconds / 1.8 km), setting the Guinness World Record for the fastest crewed rail vehicle in history—far ahead of Japan's fastest wheeled Shinkansen (E5/H5 Series at 320 km/h commercial, ALFA-X experimental at 400 km/h). The Improved L0-950 represents 80–90% of the final commercial specification for the **Chūō Shinkansen** (Tokyo Shinagawa – Nagoya in 40 minutes at **500 km/h** service speed), replacing the earlier 900-series' gas-turbine auxiliary generator (which left exhaust soot on the nose roof) with full inductive wireless power transfer from the guideway, streamlining the nose further (13% aerodynamic drag reduction), and relocating the forward optical camera and headlamp cluster into a prominent high-mounted dark visor on the nose slope. (While a single M10 intermediate car began testing in July 2025 with unpainted shark-skin riblet film, no complete M10 end-car trainset exists yet; the L0-950 is therefore the definitive, iconic blue-and-white commercial-spec Japanese 500 km/h train.)

| Gangways | Flush full-width aerodynamic outer diaphragm | Near-seamless transition between cars (~4 cm hairline seam) |

| Speeds | **500 km/h** (138.89 m/s) service; **603 km/h** (167.50 m/s) record | 180° shutter at 24 fps (1/48 s) → 2.89 m motion blur at 500 km/h, 3.49 m at 603 km/h |

## 3. Guideway & Civil Infrastructure Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Notes & Primary Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Guideway Type | **Double-track U-shaped reinforced concrete trough** | Physical derailment containment by sturdy sidewalls |
| Track Center-to-Center Spacing | **5.80 m** | Confirmed in Okumura (RTRI/JEF `cov2.2_0103.pdf`) & 2026 AIP *Physics of Fluids* HTS maglev study |
| Single U-Trough Inner Width | **~3.40 m** (3.10 m clear between coil faces) | Car width 2.90 m → ~0.10–0.15 m lateral clearance to sidewall coils |
| Sidewall Height | **~1.65 m** above trough floor | Houses white/light-grey precast FRP ground-coil panels |
| Ground Coils | Figure-8 null-flux levitation/guidance coils + LSM propulsion coils | Mounted continuously along both inner sidewalls (~0.90 m coil pitch), visible repeating dark bolt-and-loop relief pattern |
| Minimum Curve Radius | **8,000 m** (R = 8 km) | Confirmed in RTRI MAGLEV2004 (`IMT_CP_M2004_T5_17.pdf`) & MLIT Chuo Shinkansen standards |
| Maximum Cant (Superelevation) | **10°** (balances centrifugal force at 420 km/h on R = 8,000 m) | Smooth clothoid transition curves into/out of banked arcs |
| Maximum Gradient | **40 ‰ (4.0%)** | Steep gradients possible because LSM traction has no wheel-rail adhesion limit |
| Viaduct Structure | Haunched prestressed concrete box girder on arched double-column piers | Pier spacing ~30–40 m; steel inspection platforms & railings around pier caps; vertical-joint precast outer parapet walls (~1.7 m high) |
| Lightning / Comms Masts | Slender grey vertical poles (~35 m spacing) on outer parapet | No overhead catenary wires — only thin high lightning arrestor wire |
| Tunnel Portal Buffer Hoods | Slotted reinforced-concrete/steel entrance hoods (~50–60 m long) | Vent slits along roof & upper walls gradually relieve the compression wave to suppress sonic "tunnel boom" (micro-pressure wave) |
| Noise-Reduction Enclosures | Ribbed curved-roof acoustic hoods over viaduct near villages | Concrete lower wall + corrugated metal/polycarbonate arched canopy |
