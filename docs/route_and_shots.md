# Route, DEM Environments & 10-Shot Cinematography Plan

## 1. Consistent Season & Time of Day
- **Season:** Late autumn (*momiji*, mid-November) across every shot — never mixed with spring sakura.
  - Fresh snowcap on Mt Fuji (snowline ~2,350 m ASL) and Southern Alps (Minami Alps ~2,250 m ASL).
  - Deep evergreen Japanese cedar (*sugi*) and cypress (*hinoki*) slopes contrasted with crimson, burnt-orange, and golden-amber Japanese maples (*momiji*) along valley edges, river cliffs, and shrine groves.
  - Harvested golden-tan terraced rice paddy stubble with shallow rainwater reflections in Kiso; autumn vineyard trellises in Kofu Basin.
- **Time of Day:** Late-afternoon golden hour (sun elevation ~15.5°, azimuth 238° WSW, ~4,350 K direct sunlight matched to verified CC0 Poly Haven HDRI `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky_4k.exr`).
